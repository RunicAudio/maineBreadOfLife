Our Mission & Roots

Over 40 Years of Dedicated Service in Augusta

Founded in September 1984 through the visionary dedication of Carolyn Neighoff Meyer and local community members, Bread of Life has grown from a local soup kitchen into an essential regional support network in Augusta, Maine.

Our comprehensive mission is to feed the hungry, shelter the unhoused, and provide holistic support services that empower individuals and families to overcome barriers, restore dignity, and build long-term stability.

  • 2 Emergency Shelters: 40-bed family shelter and 14-bed veteran shelter on Hospital Street.
  • Downtown Soup Kitchen: Serving 26,000–39,000 hot breakfasts and lunches annually at 157 Water Street.
  • Permanent & Supportive Housing: Managing over 100 housing units across Augusta.
  • ART's House: 8-bed specialized addiction recovery and trauma facility for veterans.
Getting Help

Steps Needed to Seek Services

We provide non-discriminatory, low-barrier support to individuals and families in Kennebec County. Here is how to access our programs:

1

Pre-Intake Call

Call (207) 626-3434 ext. 4 to complete a brief pre-intake phone screening with a trained staff member.

2

Manager Review

The shelter manager reviews intake details and contacts you to discuss availability or schedule shelter arrival.

3

Shelter Arrival

Receive an assigned bed, clean bedding, towel set, personal locker, laundry access, and nutritious meals.

4

Case Planning

Work 1-on-1 with an onsite case manager to set goals, build budgets, seek employment, and secure housing vouchers.

Join Our Team

Finding Employment with Bread of Life

Build a rewarding career serving your community. Explore current openings and application steps below.

Housing Coordinator

Full-Time

Supports housing operations, rental management, tenant services, documentation, data entry, and compliance reporting. Acts as primary liaison for residents, applicants, vendors, and housing partners.

Key Requirements:

  • Degree in property management, human services, or business preferred
  • Property management / housing administration experience
  • Knowledge of Fair Housing laws & tenant rights
  • Valid Driver's License & clean driving record

Case Manager

Full-Time

Facilitates client wellness and independence through advocacy, comprehensive needs assessment, goal planning, resource navigation, and service facilitation within our shelter programs.

Key Requirements:

  • Associate's Degree in Human Services or equivalent experience
  • MHRT/C Certification required
  • Experience working with diverse populations & unhoused families
  • Valid Driver's License & clean driving record

Facilities Technician

Part-Time

Provides direct maintenance support for building/grounds repair, lawn care, snow removal, apartment turnovers, and safety checks across all Bread of Life properties.

Key Requirements:

  • High School Diploma or equivalent
  • Proficiency with hand/power tools & equipment safety
  • Apartment turnover cleaning & general repair skills
  • Valid Driver's License & clean driving record

Shelter Attendant

Full-Time / Shift Work

Ensures safe, clean, and respectful shelter environment, client intake screenings, house rule enforcement, bed checks, and hourly security inspections.

Key Requirements:

  • High School Diploma or equivalent
  • Strong interpersonal & oral communication skills
  • Compassionate demeanor with diverse client groups
  • Valid Driver's License & clean driving record

How to Apply for Employment

Bread of Life is an equal-opportunity employer offering competitive compensation, benefits, and a collaborative team environment.

  1. Prepare Your Resume: Include an updated resume and cover letter detailing your relevant experience and position interest.
  2. Email Submission: Email your resume directly to lbriggs@mainebreadoflife.org with the position title in the subject line.
  3. In-Person or Mail: Resumes can also be submitted to our Main Office at 16 East Chestnut Street, Augusta, ME 04330.
  4. Inquiries: Call our main office at (207) 626-3434 for employment questions.
